📈 [Comparing *AWS Lambda* ARM64 vs x86 Performance Across Runtimes and Node Versions](https://nodeweekly.com/link/177812/0a4070b383 "chrisebert.net") — A developer puts Node.js, Python, and Rust through their paces on Amazon’s serverless platform. On the Node side, Node 22 beats Node 20 by 8-11% across the board, but Node functions on ARM run even faster than on x86. Chris concludes: *“Switching to arm64 is the easiest performance win you can get.”* As always with benchmarks, your own results may vary.

Chris Ebert
