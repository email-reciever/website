[The State of JavaScript 2025: Backend Frameworks](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180252/0a4070b383 "2025.stateofjs.com") — The results of the popular annual JavaScript survey are out, and we’re focusing on the most relevant bit to Node.js: *backend frameworks*. [Express](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180300/0a4070b383) still leads the way, but [NestJS](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180253/0a4070b383) continues to grow rapidly. Meanwhile, [Hono](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180254/0a4070b383) comes top in developer satisfaction.

Devographics

[Node.js 25.6.0 (Current) Released](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180255/0a4070b383 "nodejs.org") — `async_hooks` can now [opt out of `Promise` tracking](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180256/0a4070b383) to cut instrumentation overhead in production. The built-in test runner supports per-run env injection (see the [`env` option here](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180257/0a4070b383)), [TextEncoder](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180258/0a4070b383) gets [some SIMD-backed speedups](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180259/0a4070b383), and the URL parser updates to Unicode 17.

[Explicit Resource Management in JavaScript](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180269/0a4070b383 "allthingssmitty.com") — You can use `using` for deterministic cleanup, calling `Symbol.dispose`/`asyncDispose` at scope exit without `try`/`finally`. 🔐 [OTPAuth: One-Time Password (HOTP/TOTP) Library](https://nodeweekly.com/link/180279/0a4070b383 "github.com") — When you log in to a site with 2FA and you’re asked for six digits from your authentication app, that’s a Time-based One-Time Password (or TOTP).
