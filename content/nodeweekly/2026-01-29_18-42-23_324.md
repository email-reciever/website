🌊 [Improving Single Executable Application Building in Node](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179985/0a4070b383 "joyeecheung.github.io") — First introduced two years ago, Node has a (still experimental) feature to [build single executable applications](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179986/0a4070b383) that can be deployed to machines that don’t have Node installed. This week’s Node.js 25.5 release, with its `--build-sea` flag, moves the final injection step into Node itself, eliminating the need for external tooling and turning what was a multi-step, low-level process into a single command.

Joyee Cheung

[Node.js 25.5.0 (Current) Released](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179987/0a4070b383 "nodejs.org") — The new `--build-sea` command-line flag is the headline feature *(see above).* `node:sqlite` also [enables defensive mode](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179988/0a4070b383) by default, and `fs.watch` gains [an `ignore` option](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179989/0a4070b383) to filter filesystem events.
