A roundup of some other interesting stories in the broader landscape:

* Whether you agree or not, Ryan Dahl, the original creator of both Node.js and Deno, drew a lot of attention for [a post on X](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179623/0a4070b383) *(above)* where he shared a thought on the shifting roles of modern software engineers in an agentic world.

* jQuery turned 20 years old last week, and then [released jQuery 4.0](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179625/0a4070b383) alongside the celebrations. The long-standing frontend utility library has now migrated entirely to ES modules making it easier to use in modern builds.

* 🕒 [Temporal Playground](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179626/0a4070b383) is an online sandbox for playing around with the [Temporal API.](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179627/0a4070b383)

* 🐘 If you're a Postgres user, I really enjoyed [this article about alternatives to 'soft delete'](https://nodeweekly.com/link/179628/0a4070b383) where rather than use a boolean or datetime flag, you could use a trigger to move a row to an archive table or capture deleted rows from the WAL for archival.
