[How We're Protecting Our Newsroom from npm Supply Chain Attacks](https://nodeweekly.com/link/178141/0a4070b383 "pnpm.io") — A software engineer at the *Seattle Times* explains how the paper has been trialing [pnpm](https://nodeweekly.com/link/178142/0a4070b383) as an alternative to `npm` specifically because of its client-side security controls.
