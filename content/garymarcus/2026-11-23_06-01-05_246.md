After a bumbling start …

… Anthropic just announced that it is aiming to IPO at “up to” $1.4 trillion (more likely a bit less), an astronomical sum for a company that has (with the possible exception of one anomalous, heavily subsidized quarter) has never made a profit and that has nearly $900 billion in future liabilities.

The Amended IPO itself is actually candid about the issues, to some degree:

The whole idea that they might be worth trillions mostly comes from projections like this absurd but popular one (try not to vomit when you look at the Y axis) made back in May, at the peak of the tokenmaxxing mania:

Predictions like that were predicated on the idea that Anthropic’s revenue would continue on the meteoric growth trajectory that tokenmaxxing drove.

The trouble is that it didn’t.

Quarter 2 growth was 147% compared to Q1, but Q3 wound up being a mere 47% compared to Q2. The decline of tokenmaxxing and the rise of good quality (if not quite top-tier) competitors from China began to take their toll.
