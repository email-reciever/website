It’s no longer just a rumor.

In a letter to Nvidia employees earlier tonight, Jensen Huang admitted that he had an affair with Alice Huang (no kinship relation so far as I know), who was involved in the chip business, perhaps trafficking in Nvidia chips, and is believed to have been a Chinese spy.

Nvidia [is investigating](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/08/nvidia-jensen-huang-investigation). As I noted here a few days ago, some members of Congress had already invited Huang to discuss the matter, even before Huang’s confession.

Much will be written about all of this. One important question is: who knew what about this and when.

At least a few people had already speculated about it a year ago, based on a photo from June 2024:

*   President Trump appears to have known about this [a year ago](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/29/us/politics/nvidia-china-jensen-huang-trump.html?smid=nytcore-ios-share), and chose to do nothing about it. Instead, [he remained close to Jensen Huang](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/trump-phones-nvidia-huang-all-in-calls-data-center-opposition-hoax.html), e.g., calling Jensen while he was on stage two months ago.
