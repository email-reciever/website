I wouldn’t urge impeaching Trump over Jensengate yet, but I would certainly investigate. And I am willing to bet the incoming Congress will feel the same way.

Here are a few things to consider:

*   Trump and Huang were very close, according to multiple media reports. At an All-In event, for example, Trump called Huang while Huang was on stage. Trump frequently referenced him and often invited to events and meetings. As more comes out about Huang and national security, this closeness may come to carry an Epstein-like taint.
    
*   Trump lied about when he had been told about Jensen Huang’s relationship with Alice Huang.
    
*   Trump apparently did nothing about it when he found about the relationship and its possible risks to national security..
    
*   Trump has not thus far fired Lutnick, who seemed to be in cahoots with Jensen’s plans to work around export controls, even though many people in both parties have called on Trump to ask for Lutnick to resign.

Why did Trump allow the sale of Nvidia chips to what appeared to be cutouts that passed them along to China? Why did he give Jensen a free pass despite their risks? Was he thinking with his portfolio, putting American tech at risk? Why hasn’t he fired Lutnick?

The incoming Congress will certainly want to get to the bottom of this. The longer Trump waits to fire Lutnick the more likely it is that he (Trump) will get impeached over the whole thing.

Given that some Republicans are China hawks, and others want to distance themselves from a lame duck who is falling fast, it’s not entirely out of the question that the Senate, now with a strong Democratic majority, could get enough votes to convict.

I am not saying all this will happen. But it definitely could.
