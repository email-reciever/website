My inbox is filled with media “think pieces” glorifying Anthropic’s IPO that were filed too soon. Many give [Dewey Beats Truman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewey_Defeats_Truman) vibes.

Because the reality is that Anthropic is on pace to have one of the most embarrassing IPOs in history. Trading, for ordinary mortals who couldn’t get an advance allocation, started at 111. By the end of the day ANT had fallen to 97. In premarket trading, it looks like Anthropic will drop another 9% or so at the open, to around 88:
