## Cool Bits

1.  Google Research [open sourced `harness2`](https://github.com/google-research/harness2), a recursive agent harness for “an open world” that allows agents to self-reflect. Is this Google’s way of subjecting agents to their performance review culture?
    
2.  [React Native shipped version 0.88](https://reactnative.dev/blog/2026/10/19/react-native-0.88) bringing variable fonts and event dispatching to the platform. It’s apparently only the third time they’ve managed to ship a release without breaking changes? Not sure I’d want to advertise this, but you do you RN.
    
3.  Convex CEO James Cowling hosted a chat with Theo Browne, Jean-Denis Greze, and David Cramer about [agentic development and the future of open source software](https://youtu.be/tU0leMxkqzE?si=PvQSgs2Ijg5JjVe9). If you haven’t watched it yet, you should. \[sponsored\]
    
4.  Speaking of libraries known for breaking changes, [React Router released version 8.5](https://reactrouter.com/changelog#v850), which deprecates its view transition APIs in favor of React’s `<ViewTransition>` component.
    
5.  Kristen Maevyn wrote [an essay on the concept of decoherence](https://maevyn.codes/decoherence) and why, according to this thing called math, AI-generated complexity could lead to our downfall.
    
6.  After getting benchmogged by all the slop forks, [the TypeScript team shipped version 7.1](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/typescript/announcing-typescript-7-1-beta/) in beta, which is 1.2–1.9x faster and uses 15–50% less memory than 7.0 in its benchmarks.
    
7.  [Blacksmith is a drop-in replacement for GitHub runners](https://www.blacksmith.sh/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=bytes_secondary_2026_10_09) that’s significantly faster and costs 60% less. It’s dead simple and also fully observable in your CI pipeline. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  Justin Bennett spent some time thinking about [how to make code reviews not suck](https://just-be.dev/blog/code-review-sucks-now/) — his words, not mine.
    
9.  Darius Cepulis asks the existential question we’ve all had anxious thoughts about — [what the iPhone Duo means for web development](https://videojs.org/blog/iphone-duo-web-development).
    
10.  Will we still have a job in 5 years? [AI.js Day 2026](https://lp.jetbrains.com/ai-js-day-2026/?utm_source=newsletter_bytes&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=webstorm_javascript_day_2026_bytes) puts frontend’s scariest AI questions on air. Free, live, happening on Nov 13. Save your spot. \[sponsored\]
    
11.  Pere Diaz Bou wrote about [what happens to a row in Turso’s MVCC](https://turso.tech/blog/mvcc-row-lifetime), from bytecode that inserts it to the checkpoint that deletes it.
    
12.  Marc-Andre Giroux finally found [a use case for GraphQL operations](https://magiroux.com/posts/from-use-case-to-graphql-operation). This is why you never let PMs tell you not to build a feature because _someone_ is going to eventually want it.
