## Cool Bits

1.  Poolside [wrote about their model factory](https://poolside.ai/blog/introducing-the-model-factory#high-level-layout-of-the-model-factory). Apparently writing Kubernetes is considered blue-collar work now.
    
2.  Lars Faye makes the case that [AI coding prevents people from becoming experts](https://larsfaye.com/articles/ai-coding-will-prevent-expertise), which is why I make my kids write assembly on scratch paper before they’re allowed to have screen time.
    
3.  Learn how Dhrumil Parekh [created an automated workflow using Sentry](https://blog.sentry.io/automated-debugging-workflow-sentry/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=paid-community&utm_campaign=seer-fy27q3-automated-debugging&utm_content=newsletter-secondary-blog-learnmore) that fixes bugs, pings reviewers, and eliminates duplicate PRs. \[sponsored\]
    
4.  [Rspack released version 2.2](https://rspack.rs/blog/announcing-2-2), featuring better performance, support for more platforms, and improvements to `import.meta`.
    
5.  The legend Kirupa wrote an article titled [Moving Around a Grid and Respecting Boundaries](https://www.kirupa.com/data_structures_algorithms/moving_around_a_grid.htm), which is an excellent resource if you want to learn how computers move objects or if you’re dating in New York.
    
6.  pnpm is now written in Rust as of [this week’s release of version 12.0.0](https://github.com/pnpm/pnpm/releases/tag/v12.0.0), which means it’s faster—but congrats, you also get some breaking changes.
    
7.  “The app feels slow” is not a bug report. EAS Observe measures render and time-to-interactive per route from real users, so you can name the screen instead of the vibe. [Free up to 100k events a month](https://d2bn2k04.na1.hs-sales-engage.com/Ctc/L0+23284/d2BN2k04/Jks2-6qcW69sMD-6lZ3lBW5Ntpw_1vt7QqW2qx-Ky8fXmwmW4r57_x1f3zStW22qTMs1MjdvGVHQMcw59r5SdW4hGs5V565svRW1dYD4C414NXpW4ZpcBw7NbMCmT35c66ZLmdqN98H1Nb4QtYXW8c7Hjb27MBcsW4hqk_m7gSK2wW28RPpy5qRHFLW2X6SbK2JVywGW7t-39y8kWlkLW2N6CLG6sQxkwW3r1tdP3444-wW17j-y-490clxW4SBXW82CR-HjW61xy5B6_MP2Gf4pWnJl04). \[sponsored\]
    
8.  Vercel Labs created `vgpu`, a [WebGPU shader library](https://vgpu.sh/) designed for agents. The fact that it’s written in TypeScript feels disrespectful, but I can’t tell who should be offended.
    
9.  Cloudflare wrote about [how they saved 100 terabytes of memory by optimizing their DNS cache](https://blog.cloudflare.com/dns-cache-memory-optimization-1111). Did I read that right? Memory?
    
10.  Hyperagent lets you build a team of specialist agents with defined jobs, shared context, and clear handoffs. They run on schedules in the cloud, pass work to one another, and bring you in when a decision requires your judgment. [Get $100 in free credits here](https://www.hyperagent.com/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=influencer&utm_campaign=FIRESHIP100). \[sponsored\]
    
11.  Flavio Copes wrote a very timely (for this newsletter) [deep dive on StyleX](https://flaviocopes.com/stylex/).
    
12.  Calvin French-Owen wrote that [small models have arrived](https://calv.info/small-models-have-arrived), and that what people really want is something that’s fast, cheap, and “good enough”. It’s also why I still eat at Taco Bell.
