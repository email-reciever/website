## Cool Bits

1.  [Christoph Nakazawa released fate 2.0](https://fate.technology/posts/whats-new-in-fate-2.0), which now supports GraphQL, Vue.js, client state management and fields that are reactive across the client / server boundary. At this rate fate 3.0 is going to have its own implementation of Unreal Engine.
    
2.  Soren Blank wrote a blog about [the history of menus and the silly invisible shape every UI library has to draw](https://sorenblank.com/craft/the-invisible-shape) to prevent users from rage-quitting your app.
    
3.  [Semgrep Guardian](https://semgrep.dev/guardian/?utm_source=fireship_bytes&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=2026-10_fireship_guardian_campaign) helps AI agents and humans catch relevant security risks and fixes them before your code ships, so you and your team can sleep easier at night. Try our Bytes-exclusive [60-day free trial](https://semgrep.dev/onboarding/guardian?offer=fireship_202610&utm_source=fireship_bytes&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=2026-10_fireship_guardian_campaign). \[sponsored\]
    
4.  Pietro Miotti wrote about [how his AI agents (re)discovered an ancient Maya site in Mexico using NASA lidar data](https://pietromiotti.com/latest/maya-site/). Lucasfilm is supposedly adapting this into the screenplay for the 6th Indiana Jones movie where Indy works from home because his sciatica prevents him from flying.
    
5.  Ryan Carniato created [a new language called ReactiveScript](https://reactivescript.dev/) for creating reactive UIs.
    
6.  Maggie Appleton wrote a post about [fortune telling and speculative design](https://maggieappleton.com/fortune-telling) which outlines tools design engineers can use to build the UIs of the future.
    
7.  [Blacksmith is a drop-in replacement for GitHub runners](https://www.blacksmith.sh/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=bytes_secondary_2026_11_13) that’s significantly faster and costs 60% less. It’s dead simple and also fully observable in your CI pipeline. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  [Alistair Smith wrote about “thenables”](https://alistair.sh/thenables), the story of what happens when your `Promise` train gets derailed because of a WebKit bug.
    
9.  [Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Small](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/), a new home-grown open-weight model that does really well on DesignArena benchmarks.
    
10.  The wait is over. [Expo Simulators](https://try.expo.dev/4zgKL9s) is officially out, so your agents can finally look at the app they keep telling you is done. Each one gets its own iOS or Android simulator, checks its work, and attaches a replay to the PR. Available now on all paid plans. \[sponsored\]
    
11.  Kath Horvak created ZigZag, free, open-source [embroidery software that lets you turn SVGs into stitches](https://zigzag.diy/).
    
12.  The GraphQL Foundation announced [a specification for GraphQL over HTTP](https://graphql.org/blog/2026-11-12-announcing-the-graphql-over-http-spec/) which hopefully means terrorists GraphQL developers will stop returning `200` for errors.
