## Cool Bits

1.  Jacob Ebey wrote [a blog about Async React and Progressive Server Actions](https://ebey.dev/blog/3myyseusgd22h). Andrew and Seb can rest easy now knowing at least one person thought their life’s work was worth it.
    
2.  [Chrome 153 has a native MP4 recorder](https://github.com/paulirish/chrome-capture) that lets you create 60–120 fps screen captures which perf legend Paul Irish turned into a tool you can use today.
    
3.  Instead of sifting through a bunch of boring bullets on GitHub, join [Sentry’s changelog livestream](https://sentry.io/resources/changelog-live/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=paid-community&utm_campaign=general-fy27q4-changeloglive&utm_content=newsletter-secondary-register) where they cover everything in their recent releases as well as a few things you might have missed. \[sponsored\]
    
4.  We’re pouring one out for Val Town, which [announced today that it is shutting down](https://blog.val.town/winding-down). The feeling of writing and running server side JS for the first time is a high I’m still chasing.
    
5.  Philip Walton wrote about [Modern Web Guidance](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/modern-web-guidance-v1), an agent skill that teaches your cl\*nker the ways of the (modern) web.
    
6.  Mitchell Hashimoto [released `libghostty` 1.0](https://mitchellh.com/writing/libghostty-1-0), the portable terminal emulator that powers terminals in JetBrains, Neovim, and all LG refrigerators as of late 2026.
    
7.  Mike from Convex made a video about [building his own open-source Agentic Development Environment](https://youtu.be/HCR6jqR0LEI). It’s called Threadbare and has some unique features like getting to your next decision as fast as possible, and syncing all threads across your Mac, browser, and phone with Convex. It’s already getting a lot of love. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  [XState v6 is now officially in beta](https://stately.ai/blog/2026-11-05-xstate-v6-beta), or for the degenerates out there, ready to use in prod.
    
9.  Vercel Labs added [support for generating Slack, Discord, and Teams cards](https://github.com/vercel-labs/json-render/releases/tag/v0.22.0) in the latest release of `json-render`.
    
10.  The CTO of Brex said that “Greptile is the only AI reviewer that doesn’t annoy the s\*\*t out of me.” [Try it out for free](https://www.greptile.com/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=sponsorship&utm_campaign=bytes_nov10) – and see if it lives up to that high praise. \[sponsored\]
    
11.  Zonder Labs wrote a blog post breaking down [why AI slop is so frustrating to read](https://zonderlabs.com/slop). I guess AI should have spent more time diagramming sentences in Mrs. Voelkel’s 7th grade English class.
