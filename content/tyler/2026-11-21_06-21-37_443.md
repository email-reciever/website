## Cool Bits

1.  [CSS and HTML support are now built in to Webpack](https://webpack.js.org/blog/2026-11-16-css-and-html-are-built-in/) which would have been amazing news 10 years ago, but like I always say, better late than never.
    
2.  Maxime Heckel wrote about [how to design lighting that “feels just right”](https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/lighting-that-feels-just-right/) in `three.js`.
    
3.  [Expo Simulators](https://try.expo.dev/4zgKL9s) let your agents spin up secure cloud simulators for iOS and Android on demand. They can install a build, check the work and attach a replay to the PR. \[sponsored\]
    
4.  Will Larson wrote about how they solved code review with [an approach they call `small-change`](https://lethain.com/small-changes/), which got my hopes up but then I realized the “solved” part was a joke.
    
5.  The Pierre boys, hot off their OpenAI acquisition, [released `@pierre/words`, a streaming Markdown renderer](https://diffs.com/words) that’s 17x faster than `markdown-it`.
    
6.  Shan Hannadige wrote about [how performance is design](https://shannadige.com/blog/performance-is-design/) now that everyone is a builder.
    
7.  The CTO of Brex said that “Greptile is the only AI reviewer that doesn’t annoy the s\*\*t out of me.” [Try it out for free](https://www.greptile.com/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=sponsorship&utm_campaign=bytes_nov10) – and see if it lives up to that high praise. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  MDO wrote about [his process for designing and coding icons](https://pierre.computer/writing/on-drawing-icons).
    
9.  Yagiz Nizipli released [version 4.1 of ada](https://github.com/ada-url/ada/releases/tag/v4.1.0), Node.js’ URL parser, making it 2x faster for parsing “real-world URLs”. Are other parsers benchmarking on fake URLs 🤔?
    
10.  [Blacksmith](https://www.blacksmith.sh/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=bytes_secondary_2026_11_20) is a drop-in replacement for GitHub runners that’s 2x faster and costs up to 67% less. It’s dead simple and also fully observable in your CI pipeline. \[sponsored\]
    
11.  shadcn created an [agent-first Tailwind linter](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/lint) that lets you write design system rules agents can verify.
    
12.  Vercel Labs created an [AI emulator](https://emulate.dev/docs/ai) for your CI and offline sandboxes which lets you test AI apps with locally scripted “deterministic but also realistic” replies. Sounds like lyrics Kent C. Dodds might use for his next [music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7jdFbg3Ijq8).
