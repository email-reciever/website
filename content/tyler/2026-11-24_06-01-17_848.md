[Sign up today](https://sentry.io/resources/otel-connected-context-workshop/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=paid-community&utm_campaign=tracing-fy27q3-otelworkshop&utm_content=newsletter-secondary-workshop-register). \[sponsored\]

5.  After 6+ years of work, Jovi De Croock and the team [released Preact 11](https://preactjs.com/blog/preact-11/), which has a new hydration system, automatic ref forwarding, and improved React compatibility. Well done 👏.
    
6.  Speaking of major releases, [Artem Zakharchenko released MSW (Mock Service Worker) v3](https://mswjs.io/blog/introducing-msw-3.0), which is now pure ESM. #courage
    
7.  For engineers leveraging coding agents, trusted infrastructure is key to security and governance. With [Coder Agents](https://fandf.co/3SQt8hP), your teams can delegate tasks and research to AI agents, all on a self-hosted platform you already trust. \[sponsored\]
