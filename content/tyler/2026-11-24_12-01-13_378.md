## Cool Bits

1.  Sam Rose wrote [a visual guide demonstrating how Kubernetes probes work](https://ngrok.com/blog/probes). Between this and my 20-year high school reunion, I think the universe is telling me to get a colonoscopy.
    
2.  David Zhang created [`jevgrep`](https://github.com/dzhng/jevgrep), a CLI that lets you search for code using natural language. `jg “What would you say...you do here?”`
    
3.  [Give your apps and AI agents real-time data from 100+ search engines using SerpApi](https://serpapi.com/bytesdev). Power your AI tools, track rankings, or discover local businesses with real-time results from Google Search, Maps, AI Overviews, and more. Get structured JSON or Markdown with one simple GET request. \[sponsored\]
    
4.  XState added [support for Effect.ts](https://stately.ai/docs/xstate/v6/effect/quick-start) that lets you combine event-driven workflows with Effect services while handling cancellation and testing. I still don’t know what Effect is, and at this point I’m too afraid to ask.
    
5.  Cloudflare released [version 1.0 of `vinext`](https://blog.cloudflare.com/vinext-nextjs-on-vite/), the OG “slopfork” that lets you run Next.js with Vite, which reminds me, we’re a little bit overdue for the next Dane vs Guillermo Twitter feud.
    
6.  Pooya Parsa created [`upm`, a fast, tiny package manager for the `npm` registry](https://upm.sh/).
    
7.  [The future of mobile development is agentic](https://try.expo.dev/4xTRl5Y). And it’s built on Expo. For years, mobile shipped slower than the web. Now agents are writing code, testing it, fixing it, shipping it OTA, and then monitoring it. All on Expo. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  The VoidZero team is still shipping post-acquisition and just released [Vite+ 1.0](https://voidzero.dev/posts/announcing-vite-plus-1-0), which combines Vite, Vitest, Oxlint, Oxfmt, Rolldown, tsdown, and Vite Task into a single package.
    
9.  [Turso 0.8.0](https://turso.tech/blog/turso-0.8.0) just got around SQLite’s single-writer bottleneck with concurrent writes.
    
10.  Domenic Denicola made [a website that pushes AI-generated content to its limit](https://domenic.me/we-didnt-start-the-scaling/). It’s weird. It makes me feel bad. But I was also kind of impressed?
