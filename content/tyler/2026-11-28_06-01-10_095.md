## Cool Bits

1.  The TanStack family grew again with [their latest child package, Hotkeys](https://tanstack.com/hotkeys/latest), which provides a type-safe, framework-agnostic way to add keyboard shortcuts to your app.
    
2.  React Native [released version `0.87`](https://reactnative.dev/blog/2026/08/11/react-native-0.87), which features a “strict” TypeScript API, a Metro update, and the realization that I’ll be in my 80s when they hit `1.0.0`.
    
3.  The new [Software Stewardship Lab](https://stewardshiplab.org/ideas/a-research-lab-for-open-source/) is raising funds to help sustain the open-source maintainers and software supply chains the industry depends on. \[sponsored\]
    
4.  Ben Vinegar created [Hunk, a terminal diff viewer](https://www.hunk.dev/) built to make reviewing agent code more manageable.
    
5.  Vicki Boykis wrote [an essay about AI-generated PR descriptions](https://vickiboykis.com/2026/08/12/write-for-people/) and why you, sir / ma’am, should just write it yourself.
    
6.  Nathan Sobo wrote about [software craftsmanship in the era of vibes](https://zed.dev/blog/software-craftsmanship-in-the-era-of-vibes).
    
7.  [OpenRouter gives you one API to access 400+ models](https://openrouter.ai/?utm_source=fireship&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=2026-09_dev-sponsorships&utm_content=description). You get better prices, better uptime, and no subscriptions to worry about. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  [DeepSeek got a lot more expensive](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/). I thought inference was free in China wtf.
    
9.  Florian Herrengt wrote about [the disappearing middle class in software engineering](https://blog.florianherrengt.com/ai-removing-middle-class-software-engineering.html). Late-stage capitalism is coming for everything smh.
    
10.  Lea Verou explains why you can skip [the tri-state toggle for “dark mode”](https://lea.verou.me/blog/2026/dark-mode-toggles/) on your personal site. And no, it’s not because no one visits it, but that’s also true.
