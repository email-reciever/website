## Cool Bits

1.  Juri wrote about [building maintainable Angular apps at scale](https://nx.dev/blog/architecting-angular-applications). We’ll never understand the pain that went into gaining this knowledge.
    
2.  Philip Zeyliger officially [declared the end of the “no-code” era of software](https://blog.exe.dev/the-end-of-no-code). Pouring one out for the Airtable / Zapier stack I ran my business on for the last decade.
    
3.  Your app is slower on a mid-range Android in a parking garage than it is on your desk, and [Observe](https://d2bn2k04.na1.hs-sales-engage.com/Ctc/L0+23284/d2BN2k04/JkM2-6qcW6N1vHY6lZ3kMW8gCgz72ZdN7XW3Rsgn-3nCW4pW34nVpK6MSYyCW1cF96h5QyjRwW7ctl9d7Hn5CtW1SsVbC4gR8hXW6n_Q711mvvhMW2DyYwc7_tM1FVqMBdS5p1mfyN11yfPjfR0NlV7xx0h67GqYLW11MHc35rJqfZW8Ff3V59jR9_7VvD6Yl8xXGZrW2z5XLX8wXy88W2tv9Lx1V2CzCW1RLzs96HFwL7VlvHpD7zP6vPW8GHlyh9fC0-vW2WvF1S7TXz1RW2hPxr57Kx1WJW5s5H-Q1H8WR_f2yqJrl04) is Expo’s way of showing you exactly how much, and which release caused the problems. \[sponsored\]
    
4.  Tyler Sticka wrote about [the new and improved `text-stroke` CSS property](https://tylersticka.com/journal/improved-css-text-stroke/). I am equal parts excited and scared for what Lynn Fisher is going to do with this.
    
5.  Emil Kowalski argues that [the design process needs more friction](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/friction-as-a-feature). Feels a little too similar to Trojan’s ribbed condom campaign, but I’ll take his word for it.
    
6.  Senko Rašić [took offense to people saying “code was never the hard part”](https://blog.senko.net/code-was-never-the-hard-part-is-an-insult-to-all-programmers). I too, am offended because of coding was never the hard part, why did a trailing space in wp-config.php accelerate my hair loss?
    
7.  Only idiots write manual tests – modern engineering teams like Notion, Dropbox and LaunchDarkly use Meticulous to maintain e2e UI tests that [cover every edge case](https://www.meticulous.ai/?utm_source=bytes&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=26q3&utm_content=secondary) of your web app. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  Dan Luu did some research to figure out [the best programming language for LLMs](https://danluu.com/pl-tokens/).
    
9.  Fresh off getting acquired by Qualcomm, Chris Lattner and co [released version 1.0 of Mojo](https://www.modular.com/blog/modular-26-5-mojo-1-0-is-here), the python-like programming language for AI hardware.
    
10.  Jared Sumner [got Claude to make real progress on one of Math’s hardest problems](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta) by telling it “keep going” and “believe in yourself”. Will he win the first Nobel prize for words of affirmation?
