AI is changing how we design and build, and it isn’t slowing down. On December 1, 2026, SmashingConf is hosting the [AI 2027 Conference](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference), a live online half day event to take stock of where AI stands today, what we can expect in 2027, and where teams are getting it right.

With AI maturing, it’s clear we need to learn how AI can elevate your workflow as a UX designer and Design System engineer. Most of us are still figuring it out and trying to work smarter in this new world, so we invited industry experts who have been doing this for a while. They show their experience and tell you **what works and what doesn't**, so you don’t have to figure it all out yourself.

Every session is practical. Speakers show how they actually work: their tools, their setups, and the small things that are easy to miss. The event ends with moderated breakout rooms, where **you can bring your own workflow questions**. [See you there?](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/registration)

About AI 2027 Conf And Schedule

This conference is dedicated to designers and engineers (front-end, UI, UX) who want to understand how AI can improve the way their team works, without the hype. If you’re interested in AI-ready design systems, AI and Figma, harness engineering, agentic AI UX patterns, and AI workflows for design and engineering teams, this conference is right up your alley!

**Showing, not telling**: All sessions will be highly practical, focusing on how to integrate AI in your daily work and what to expect in 2027.

07:45 AM — Doors Open

8:00 AM — Welcome!

+

For our AI 2027 Conference, your friendly host [Vitaly](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/vitaly-friedman/) will welcome you and quickly explain how the event will run. We can’t wait to meet you!

8:10 AM — Designing AI Experiences in 2027

+

AI is moving fast. With it come new workflows in product teams, new design patterns, new user expectations and new ways of thinking about building and designing for AI.  
  
Let’s look at where things stand today. A lot changed in 2026, and there are things we can now design and build that simply weren’t possible a year ago. We’ll cover the patterns taking shape heading into 2027, from multi-modal experiences to accessibility, and what tends to go wrong along the way. A big part of that is designing for trust, so people feel confident using what we make.  
  
What makes AI products reliable in practice? We’ll look at how teams cut down on hallucinations with better context, a well-built harness and sensible guardrails. Then we’ll finish with what’s coming in 2027, including new experiments, regulations and a few takeaways to bring back to your team.  
  
Roll up your sleeves for a packed session, full of real-life examples and design patterns.

8:45 AM — Prototyping AI Functionality

+

Great AI-powered products live at the intersection of engineering depth, design craft and product thinking. In this talk, [Anna Arteeva](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/anna-arteeva/) shows how to build real, working AI experiences, not just one-shot prototypes that fall apart the moment someone uses them.  
  
You’ll learn how to design and prototype an AI-powered experience from scratch, with functionality built on LLMs, voice and image generation logic. Anna will also show how to add that little bit of delight to your app, and how it can respond to different types of input, whatever the person on the other side of the screen gives you: text, voice or images.

9:20 AM — Nobody Will Read Your Documentation

+

What coding agents need from your design system docs, and how to write it.  
  
Documentation is the part of the design system teams are least happy with, and it’s been that way for years. [Luke Murphy](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/luke-murphy/) looks at how we ended up with docs nobody reads and why agents change the picture. Then, drawing on DS ARC’s tests of real design systems, he walks through the small writing changes that make docs work for agents and people alike.  
  
_This is a talk from our friends at [zeroheight](https://zeroheight.com/?utm_campaign=59260066-Smashing%20Conf%20AI%20Event%20-%20MKTG.EXTEVENT.NOV.26&utm_source=smashing_event_AI). Thank you for joining in!_

9:40 AM — Design Systems and Figma MCPs

+

The big question for many teams is how to get your code and your Figma file talking to each other. In this session, [Christine Vallaure](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/christine-vallaure/) shares how she has set this up in practice: going from code to Figma instead of the other way around, moving back and forth between the two for the best result, and what that means for how we work.  
  
You’ll hear why design system basics suddenly matter a lot more, why naming things well pays off, and why AI builders so often produce code that looks like your design system but still has to be rebuilt. She’ll also explain the engineering side that designers don’t always see, like the checks and files your developers set up, so you know how it works and what to ask for. Along the way, she covers the problems you’re likely to run into, what to keep in mind, and where Storybook fits in.  
  
Expect plenty of small tips and tricks you can take straight back to your team.

10:15 AM — Alternative Workflows for AI and Design Systems

+

AI flipped the script on the traditional, linear design system workflow. That’s okay. We’re all experimenting, and nobody has this figured out yet.  
  
[TJ Pitre](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/tj-pitre/) has spent the past two years working with dozens of teams to find the flow that fits them. He’ll show three alternative workflows live. Which one fits depends on you: how big your design system is, how big your team is, how much effort you’re willing to put in, and how well it needs to scale.  
  
The goal is to get you thinking, and to send you back to your team with a few workflows worth trying.

10:50 AM — Real-World AI & Design Systems Workflow

+

[Brad Frost](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/brad-frost/) shares his actual workflow for building products with AI and a design system. He’ll show the setup, architecture, and tools he uses to keep AI on the rails of his components, tokens, and patterns.  
  
Expect live building, real design decisions, and plenty of practical tricks along the way. You’ll see how it all works together and leave with ideas to apply to your own design system and workflow.

### Anna Arteeva

[Anna Arteeva](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/anna-arteeva/) is a product design leader turned agentic coding instructor, and one of Maven’s top-ranked educators, formerly head of product design at Payoneer. With a technical background (frontend), a career in product design, and a healthy obsession with building things. She found her calling in helping designers unlock their superpowers with coding agents, helping designers move beyond AI hype and one-shot prototypes, and develop the technical fluency, practical skills, and good judgement to make agentic coding a powerful part of professional design practice.

*   Anna Arteeva’s conf talk: [Prototyping AI Functionality](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/anna-arteeva/)

![](https://files.smashing.media/articles/ai-conf/ai-conf-anna-arteeva.png)

### Christine Vallaure

[Christine Vallaure](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/christine-vallaure/) is a designer and educator with twenty years in product work. She is the founder of Moonlearning.io, a learning platform that helps designers blur the line between design and code, and the author of _The Solo_. Christine spends most of her time in the developers’ weeds and brings back what designers actually need, then turns it into far too detailed articles, hands-on teaching and talks at conferences around the world.

*   Christine Vallaure’s conf talk: [Design Systems and Figma MCPs](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/christine-vallaure/)

![](https://files.smashing.media/articles/ai-conf/ai-conf-christine.png)

### TJ Pitre

[TJ Pitre](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/tj-pitre/) is the Product Architect for AI and Design Systems at Figma, and Founder/Owner of Southleft, a design systems consultancy. He works with teams to bridge design and engineering using structured context, modern tooling, and practical experimentation.

*   TJ Pitre’s conf talk: [Starting Anywhere: Alternative Workflows for AI and Design Systems](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/tj-pitre/)

![](https://files.smashing.media/articles/ai-conf/ai-conf-TJ.png)

### Brad Frost

[Brad Frost](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/brad-frost/) is a design system consultant, web designer & developer, speaker, writer, teacher, musician, and artist located in beautiful Pittsburgh, PA. He helps people establish & evolve design systems, establish more collaborative workflows, and design & build software together. Brad is the author of the book _Atomic Design_, which introduces a methodology to create and maintain effective design systems. He co-hosted the _Style Guides Podcast_ and has helped create several tools and resources for web designers, e.g. an online course, [AI & Design Systems](https://aianddesign.systems/), with experts from the industry.

*   Brad Frost’s conf talk: [Real-World AI & Design Systems Workflow](https://smashingconf.com/ai-2027-conference/speakers/brad-frost/)
