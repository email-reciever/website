📢  Elsewhere in the ecosystem

Some other interesting stories in the broader landscape:

* ⚠️ The npm ecosystem is [currently undergoing a new wave of 'Shai Hulud' attacks](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177641/5d0bbb4a61) with post-install scripts of affected packages exfiltrating tokens, as well as infecting and republishing other packages.

* ⚠️ GitHub reports secrets/tokens 'leaked' in Git repos to service providers so they can be revoked, but it's now [reporting secrets in unlisted GitHub Gist posts too](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177642/5d0bbb4a61) (no, they're *not* truly private).

* I enjoyed [Colin Eberhardt's write up of using GitHub's *Spec Kit*](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177643/5d0bbb4a61) for building a modern front-end app. His app is built in Svelte but most of what's covered can apply to React, too.

* [Google unveiled Angular v21](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177644/5d0bbb4a61) last week, the latest version of its popular JavaScript framework. I enjoyed the [nifty retro gaming themed tour](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177645/5d0bbb4a61) of v21's new features.

* Ecma's TC39 committee convened last week and progressed numerous proposals including [Iterator Sequencing](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177646/5d0bbb4a61), [Await dictionary of Promises](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177647/5d0bbb4a61), [Joint Iteration](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177648/5d0bbb4a61), [Iterator Join](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177649/5d0bbb4a61), and [Typed Array Find Within](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177650/5d0bbb4a61).

* 🎤 TypeScript's Daniel Rosenwasser and Jake Bailey [went on the TypeScript.fm podcast](https://react.statuscode.com/link/177651/5d0bbb4a61) to talk about what's coming up in TypeScript 6 and 7.
