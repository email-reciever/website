I don’t bother myself with numbers. I rarely look at views, impressions, likes, and metrics because I don’t want to optimize for quantity but quality. But I’ve written my fair share of words this year, and I’ve got little more to spare, so I’ll use mostly numbers this time around and see what story they tell.

*   12 newsletters published - [Fragments of Code](https://fragmentsofcode.substack.com/)
*   My most read programming article was about distributed systems - [Link](https://fragmentsofcode.substack.com/p/the-intro-to-distributed-systems)
*   2 blog posts on my personal blog
*   One of them went viral - [I know when you’re vibe coding](https://alexkondov.com/i-know-when-youre-vibe-coding/)
*   14 card game articles - [The Sideboard](https://thesideboard.substack.com/)
*   0 articles written with AI
*   1 book published - [Full-Stack Tao](https://www.fullstacktao.com/)
*   7 short story drafts written in Bulgarian (to be published next year)
*   6 months of everyday writing (yes, every day)
*   2 articles written but never published
*   1 purpose found

We live in interesting times.

It’s somehow both the best time for humanity, but it feels like we’re in the first pages of a sci-fi novel where the author is telling how it all went wrong.

I live in a pocket of calm and normalcy in a world that seems chaotic, like a hobbit in the Shire, thinking about holidays and music while the shadow is rising. But Gandalf’s words kept ringing in my ears.

> All we have to decide is what we do with the time that is given us.

I kept looking for that thing people call purpose. Something I’m good at, that brings me joy, and is also useful to others. At this point in time this seems to be writing.

*   32 books read

I felt I wasn’t reading enough, and I was surprised when I counted that many in my Goodreads profile. I developed a habit to carry a book in my bag in case I dropped out of a card game tournament (more on that later), and I can flip a few pages here and there.

*   24 Programming YouTube ideos - [My Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@alexkondov)
*   2900 YouTube subscribers
*   My most watched video (17K views) was about cleaning up AI messes - [Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BBq_Ie2ed7A)

Difficult times create an atmospheric pressure that can force your ideals out of you. But if people can be reading Plato in the bread lines in Yugoslavia, then we can strive for a higher form of existence even when it all seems a bit dystopian.

I started recording videos as an alternative medium for my writing so it can reach more people, and I fell in love with it. I started enjoying recording and editing.

*   1 software product shipped
*   2 consulting clients declined (because of health)

I started prioritizing my health and writing seriously. When I was consulting, I was doing 12-hour days, waking up each night ready to vomit out of anxiety.

*   ~720 anxiety pills taken
*   3 panic attacks
*   3 nights spent watching the stars on the porch
*   1 flight in a storm survived
*   2 months away from social media
*   3 opportunities declined (talks and podcasts)
*   6 therapy sessions

In the last decade, I was using my body as an instrument to train my willpower, and I finally reached its limits. Anxiety pills can do wonders, but they make you fall asleep at the worst possible times, and it may seem impolite to take a nap in the office while people are talking to you.

I never believed I could get on a flight again after I started having panic attacks last year, and I got a trial by fire because we took off in a storm. It shook so much that the lady in the seat behind me passed out. I was playing Slay the Spire on my phone to take my mind off what was happening, but I was glad I went to the toilet before we took off.

*   9 card game tournaments entered
*   5 of them dropped
*   3 times in the top cut (including Bulgarian nationals)
*   1 tournament won

Card games have been a way to detach my mind from reality. They scratch the competitive itch and give the intellectual stimulation of playing a complex game like chess. But they also have beautiful fantasy art which makes them more fun than poker.

*   2 games designed
*   0 games published

The time I spend with card games made me excited about game design, and I spent a couple of months dabbling into it during the summer. I designed a card game of my own and a rules-light RPG, but pursuing this further would mean sacrificing writing time and I just can’t do that.

*   1 year I refused to burn for output
*   1 year I stopped stealing from future me
*   1 year I treated myself as a long-term project

Here’s to a good year.
