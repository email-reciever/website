[jQuery 4.0 Released](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179443/d076523b1d "blog.jquery.com") — 20 years on from its original release, the ever-popular ([in terms of actual usage](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179444/d076523b1d)) library reaches 4.0 with a migration to ES modules (compatible with modern build tools) along with dropping support for IE 10 and older. With jQuery being a popular guest in our newsletters in the early years, it’s fantastic to see it pop back for a quick visit.
