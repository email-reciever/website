**RELEASES:**

* [Node.js 25.5.0 (Current)](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179793/d076523b1d) – Introduces a `--build-sea` option that simplifies the process of building single executable applications.

* [Bun v1.3.7](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179794/d076523b1d) – The popular runtime updates its JavaScriptCore engine, leading to 35% faster `async`/`await` and ARM64 perf improvements. It also lands a new option to generate profiling data in Markdown format for easier sharing, plus native JSON5 and JSONL parsing support.

* [Rolldown 1.0 RC](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179834/d076523b1d) – Fast Rust-based bundler with a Rollup-compatible API and esbuild feature parity.

* [npm v11.8.0](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179835/d076523b1d), [Emscripten 5.0](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179797/d076523b1d), [Neutralinojs 6.5.0](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/179798/d076523b1d)
