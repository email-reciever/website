[Deno is Joining Cloudflare (But…)](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191449/d076523b1d "deno.com") — Maybe David Bushell [saw the writing on the wall](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191450/d076523b1d), as the Deno team isn't joining Cloudflare to keep working on *Deno*, but to fold [celld](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191451/d076523b1d) into `workerd` so Durable Objects can be self-hosted ([more on that here](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191506/d076523b1d)). The Deno runtime will get a final year of official fixes, Deno Deploy closes in six months, and [JSR](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191452/d076523b1d) moves to Cloudflare's infrastructure.

Ryan Dahl

💡 [On Hacker News](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191453/d076523b1d), Ryan Dahl noted that Deno *"has been sucked into the gravity well of Node compatibility"* before asking: *"Why reimplement Node? It works."* But what happens [to the JavaScript™ trademark case](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191454/d076523b1d), I wonder…?

[Bun 1.4.3 Adds `bun check`, a Built-In TypeScript Type Checker](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191455/d076523b1d "bun.com") — `bun check` is a port of TypeScript 7's checker that reads your `tsconfig.json`, reports the same errors as `tsc` and, Jarred claims, runs 3-6x faster. It plugs into `bun run`/`test`/`build` too. Idle server processes now use far less CPU, and a JavaScriptCore upgrade picks up [numerous perf gains](https://javascriptweekly.com/link/191456/d076523b1d).
