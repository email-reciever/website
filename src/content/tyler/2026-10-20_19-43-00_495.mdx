## Cool Bits

1.  Google Research [open sourced `harness2`](https://c5e21242.click.convertkit-mail.com/mvu5nv5m3wa5hqr63z4bmhrr87mkks3hekq6/3ohphdu38904d7brhn/aHR0cHM6Ly9naXRodWIuY29tL2dvb2dsZS1yZXNlYXJjaC9oYXJuZXNzMg==), a recursive agent harness for “an open world” that allows agents to self-reflect. Is this Google’s way of subjecting agents to their performance review culture?
    
2.  [React Native shipped version 0.88](https://c5e21242.click.convertkit-mail.com/mvu5nv5m3wa5hqr63z4bmhrr87mkks3hekq6/n2hohquv45m6ovu6hg/aHR0cHM6Ly9yZWFjdG5hdGl2ZS5kZXYvYmxvZy8yMDI2LzEwLzE5L3JlYWN0LW5hdGl2ZS0wLjg4) bringing variable fonts and event dispatching to the platform. It’s apparently only the third time they’ve managed to ship a release without breaking changes? Not sure I’d want to advertise this, but you do you RN.
    
3.  Convex CEO James Cowling hosted a chat with Theo Browne, Jean-Denis Greze, and David Cramer about [agentic development and the future of open source software](https://c5e21242.click.convertkit-mail.com/mvu5nv5m3wa5hqr63z4bmhrr87mkks3hekq6/48hvh7um3qv626axh7/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cueW91dHViZS5jb20vd2F0Y2g_c2k9UHZRU2dzMklqZzVKalZlOSZ2PXRVMGxlTXhrcXpF). 6. 7. It’s dead simple and also fully observable in your CI pipeline. \[sponsored\]
    
8.  Justin Bennett spent some time thinking about [how to make code reviews not suck](https://c5e21242.click.convertkit-mail.com/mvu5nv5m3wa5hqr63z4bmhrr87mkks3hekq6/l2heh6ulpve6k7u6h0/aHR0cHM6Ly9qdXN0LWJlLmRldi9ibG9nL2NvZGUtcmV2aWV3LXN1Y2tzLW5vdy8=) — his words, not mine.
    
9. 10.  Will we still have a job in 5 years?
